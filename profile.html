Player Profile

Full name
Mahendra Singh Dhoni
Born
7 July 1981, Ranchi, Bihar (now Jharkhand), India
Role
Wicketkeeper-batter (former captain, all formats)
Batting style
Right-handed

Debuts & Retirement — All Formats

  • Test debut: 2 December 2005 vs Sri Lanka, Chennai — retired: December 2014 (after 3rd Test vs Australia, Melbourne)
  • ODI debut: 23 December 2004 vs Bangladesh, Chittagong — retired: 15 August 2020 (announced retirement from all international cricket)
  • T20I debut: 1 December 2006 vs South Africa, Johannesburg — retired: 15 August 2020
  • International retirement (all formats): announced 15 August 2020 via social media

🏏 International Records (Tests, ODIs, T20Is)

  • 538 matches played (all as wicket-keeper) – most by any wicket-keeper
  • 332 international matches captained – most ever
  • 17,266 international runs – second most by a wicket-keeper
  • 4,876 Test runs – most by an Indian wicket-keeper
  • 183* – highest ODI score by a wicket-keeper
  • 224 – only Test double-century by an Indian wicket-keeper (vs Australia, 2013)
  • 229 ODI sixes, 359 total international sixes – among top six overall
  • 84 ODI not-outs – most in ODI history
  • Second most ODI wins as captain (110); second-most matches captained (200)
  • Most stumpings in ODIs (123)
  • All-format stumpings: 195, most in cricket history
  • International dismissals: 829 total (catches + stumpings)
  • Fastest stumping in cricket: 0.08 seconds (vs Keemo Paul, 2018)
  • Most T20I stumpings (34) and dismissals in a T20I innings (5)
  • Most T20I matches as captain (72)
  • Most catches in a Test career by an Indian keeper (256 catches, 38 stumpings)

IPL & Franchise Records

  • 278 IPL matches played (most ever)
  • 212 matches captained in IPL with ~60.95% win rate
  • Five IPL titles: 2010, 2011, 2018, 2021, 2023
  • Most IPL wicket-keeper dismissals: 201 (153 catches, 47 stumpings)
  • CSK runs: 4,715 runs with 252 sixes for Chennai Super Kings
  • Seasons with strike rate >120, including 220.55 in 2024
