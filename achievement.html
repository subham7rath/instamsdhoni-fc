ICC Trophies (as Captain)

2007 — ICC T20 World Cup Champion

Date: September 2007  |  Place: Johannesburg, South Africa

Led a young Indian side to victory in the inaugural ICC T20 World Cup, beating Pakistan in a nail-biting final.

2011 — ICC Cricket World Cup Champion

Date: 2 April 2011  |  Place: Wankhede Stadium, Mumbai

Promoted himself up the order and finished the final unbeaten on 91, hitting the winning six to end India's 28-year wait for the World Cup.

2013 — ICC Champions Trophy Winner

Date: June 2013  |  Place: England

Became the first captain to win all three ICC white-ball trophies (T20 WC, ODI WC, Champions Trophy).

IPL Titles (Chennai Super Kings)

2010, 2011, 2018, 2021, 2023 — IPL Champion with CSK

Titles: 5  |  Team: Chennai Super Kings

Led CSK to five IPL titles across more than a decade, making him the most successful captain in IPL history.

Civilian & Military Honours

2009 — Padma Shri

Date: 2009  |  Place: New Delhi, India

Awarded the Padma Shri, India's fourth-highest civilian honour.

2018 — Padma Bhushan

Date: April 2018  |  Place: New Delhi, India

Awarded the Padma Bhushan, India's third-highest civilian honour.

2011 — Honorary Lieutenant Colonel, Indian Territorial Army

Date: November 2011

Conferred the honorary rank of Lieutenant Colonel in the Parachute Regiment of the Indian Territorial Army.

Individual Honours

2008 — Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna

Date: August 2008

India's highest sporting honour, awarded for his performances as captain and batter.

2025 — Inducted into the ICC Cricket Hall of Fame

Date: 9 June 2025  |  Place: London, UK

Recognised for a career spanning 538 international matches, 17,266 runs and 829 dismissals.
